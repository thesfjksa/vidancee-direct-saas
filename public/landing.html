FAQ

Questions, answered

How do credits work?

Each plan adds credits to your account. Every video, image, voice or upscale shows its price in credits before you start, and the credits are taken only when you create.

What happens if a generation fails?

Your credits are refunded automatically, and the reason is shown on the job.

Can I use my own product photo?

Yes. Add photos as references in Video studio or Image studio, or use one as the first frame of your video on models that support it.

Which video sizes can I make?

Video studio makes 720p and 1080p in shapes such as 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 and 4:3. The Upscaler can take a finished video to 2K or 4K.

Can I use CMM Tools from Claude?

Yes. After you sign in, open Use with Claude and click Add to Claude. Then ask Claude for a video or image and it appears right in the chat.
