Add to Claude
Opens Claude with CMM Tools filled in: click Add, then Connect, sign in here and choose Allow.
  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, click + Add and choose Add custom connector. (CMM Tools isn’t in Claude’s connector search; it is added by URL.)
  2. Name it CMM Tools, paste the URL above, and click Add.
  3. Click Connect, sign in with your CMM Tools account and choose Allow.
  4. In a chat, turn on CMM Tools under + → Connectors and ask, for example: “Make a 5-second 9:16 Seedance 2.5 ad from my product photo.”
Claude Code: run claude mcp add --transport http cmm-tools …, then /mcp to sign in.
